What is recorded while monitoring is ON
  • Switching tabs, opening new tabs, closing the exam tab
  • Leaving the platform's approved websites (host name only)

Never recorded: screen, camera, microphone, keystrokes, clipboard, passwords, answers, page content or other browsing history.

These are technical signals, not proof of misconduct.
